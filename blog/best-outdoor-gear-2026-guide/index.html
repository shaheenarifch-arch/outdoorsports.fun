Camp comfort

Warm-weather camping is easier with a way to cool the tent and keep food cold. A portable tent air conditioner needs a power source, so check its power draw against your generator or campsite hook-up. Pair it with a cooler or portable fridge, a light tent you can carry, and a sleeping bag rated for the lowest temperature you expect.

Frequently asked questions

What outdoor gear should I buy first?

Start with the gear that affects comfort and safety most for your activity: shelter and sleep system for camping, a proper rack for transporting bikes, and weather-appropriate layers for hunting.

Do portable tent air conditioners need a generator?

They need a power source, such as a generator, a large power station or a campsite electrical hook-up. Check the unit's power requirements before you buy.

Is a walking pad good for small apartments?

Yes. Walking pads are low-profile and many fold or slide under a bed or sofa. Check the weight limit and noise level first.
